About Priya Art Studio

Where Creativity Meets Your Everyday Style

Welcome to Priya Art Studio — a creative lifestyle brand from Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Priya Art Studio was born from a simple love for creativity, beautiful details and meaningful things. Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, we bring together three things we love the most — handcrafted Resin Art, stylish T-Shirts and elegant Jewellery.

What started with a passion for creating beautiful handmade pieces has grown into a space where art, fashion and personal style come together.

🎨 Our Resin Art

We believe that some memories are too special to be kept only in photographs. From wedding varmala preservation and memory frames to personalized resin keepsakes and keychains, we turn your precious moments into something you can treasure for years.

👕 Our T-Shirts

Fashion should be comfortable, expressive and fun. Our T-shirts are selected and created with a focus on everyday style, beautiful designs and easy-to-wear looks that let you express your personality.

💎 Our Jewellery

A little sparkle can make any look special. Our jewellery collection brings together elegant, feminine and versatile pieces designed to complement both everyday outfits and special occasions.

❤️ Made With Love, From Ahmedabad

Being an Ahmedabad-based brand, we are proud to bring our creativity from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, to customers across India.

Every order is more than just a product for us. Whether it's a resin piece preserving an unforgettable memory, a T-shirt that represents your personality, or a jewellery piece that completes your look — we want every Priya Art Studio creation to feel special to you.

Our Promise

Beautiful products. Thoughtful details. Personal touches.

We are continuously exploring new ideas, designs and creations to make Priya Art Studio a place where you can discover something beautiful, meaningful and uniquely yours.
